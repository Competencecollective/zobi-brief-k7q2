How a 30-day test works

  1. Connect Shopify. Zobi reads checkouts and orders from your store. Nothing changes on your site.
  2. Start with abandoned checkouts. That is 71% of the money on this page and the cleanest thing to measure. Same offer you already use in email, so the comparison is the conversation, not a bigger discount.
  3. Read it in your own Shopify. Every order Zobi claims is an order in your admin, tied to a conversation you can open and read. Judge it on revenue per shopper texted and opt-out rate against the numbers above.
